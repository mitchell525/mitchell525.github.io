Little resin ducks, frogs, snails, turtles and hedgehogs are tucked away all over the house: in mugs, under beds, behind books and inside drawers. Look around each room, open things up and turn out the lights to catch the ones that glow. Every animal comes in plain, glitter, clear jelly and glow, with a few rare golden ones on top, so there are 100 to collect. Turn each one over in your collection and set your favourites out on the windowsill shelf.

**Do I need to download anything?** No. It's a free YouTube Playable, so it plays right in the YouTube app or your
browser, on phone or computer.
