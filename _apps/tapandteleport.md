You are a lone wizard with exactly one spell: teleport. Tap anywhere in the arena and you are there. Now survive.

Tap and Teleport is a 3D reaction game. Fireballs, archers, soldiers, lancers and rival mages pour into the
arena, and the only way through is to blink out of the way at the last moment. Every teleport drains energy,
so collect the glowing orbs to recharge, score points and pick up power-ups. Hesitate and you are done;
teleport well and the run keeps going.

Everything in one place:
- One-touch control — tap to teleport, nothing else to learn
- A growing cast of hazards, each with its own timing to read
- Energy management: every blink costs, every orb refills
- Power-ups that turn a close call into a comeback
- Endless survival with a saved high score
- A Game Center leaderboard for your best run
- Offline single player, no account needed
- Optional one-time Remove Ads purchase

Rebuilt and back on the App Store:
Tap and Teleport was rebuilt for current iPhones and iPads (iOS 15 or later), with the ads and privacy
frameworks brought up to date. It is free with a small banner ad, and the NO ADS button opens a one-time
$2.99 Remove Ads purchase that turns off every ad for good. Restore Purchases is built in.
