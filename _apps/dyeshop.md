Customers come into Mo the cat's shop with an order: a spiral, a bullseye or stripes, in the colors they want. Fold or band the shirt, drop the dye where it needs to go, then unfold it for the reveal and see how close you got. Good matches earn coins and stars, which unlock new dyes and clothes, and every shirt you make goes into your closet to keep.

**Do I need to download anything?** No. It's a free YouTube Playable, so it plays right in the YouTube app or your
browser, on phone or computer.
