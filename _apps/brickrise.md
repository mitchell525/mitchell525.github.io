Drop balls. Break bricks. Hold the line.

Brickrise is a one-tap brick breaker with a roguelite run. The dropper sweeps the rail on its own; tap, or hold,
to drop balls through the pegs into numbered bricks rising from the dark. Every hit counts a brick down. Let one
reach the rim and it costs you a barrier, and when the last of three is gone, the run is over.

Every run is different:
- One-tap play: no aiming, just timing
- Level up and pick 1 of 3 upgrades, from ball storms to missiles
- Bomb bricks that chain, ice that freezes the rise, and chests with a free pick
- A Crystal Titan boss every fifth level
- Unlock fun balls, each with its own best score
- Neon look and an original soundtrack made for the game
- A Game Center leaderboard for your best Star ball run
- Offline single player, no account needed
- Optional one-time Remove Ads purchase

Remove Ads:
The game is free with a small banner ad. If you would rather play without it, a one-time Remove Ads purchase
turns off every ad for good. It is a purchase, not a subscription, and Restore Purchases is built in.
