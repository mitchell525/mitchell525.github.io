The last car each lap is out. Don't be the last car.

Knockout Racer is an elimination race through a bright, sunny city. Eight cars line up on the grid. Every lap, whoever
is in last place is knocked out of the race, so you can drive clean and stay ahead, or shove a rival into a barrier and
send them out early. Win the race and you move on to the next city, with tougher rivals waiting.

Between laps you choose an upgrade and it bolts straight onto your car: rocket launchers on the roof, a cow catcher on
the front, a disco horn. Start in the Interceptor and earn coins to unlock the Brute, the Dart, the Party Van, the Big
Rig and more.

- Hold and drag to steer, one thumb
- A new city after every win
- 21 upgrades to combine, from sensible to completely unhinged
- Coins and car unlocks that carry from run to run
- A Game Center leaderboard for your best run
- Portrait or landscape, and made for iPhone Duo
