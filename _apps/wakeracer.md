Eight speedboats, three laps, and a pack that stays right on your tail. Drag to carve, launch off the big swells and land on a rival's deck to send them spinning. Every last lap brings a storm, and the Coast Cup ends on Volcano Isle, where the volcano erupts while you race. Score points across each three-race cup, take the trophy, and unlock new boats along the way: eleven in all, each with its own hull, from the Coast Cup through to the Grid Cup.

**Do I need to download anything?** No. It's a free YouTube Playable, so it plays right in the YouTube app or your
browser, on phone or computer.
