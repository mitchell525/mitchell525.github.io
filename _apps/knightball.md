Your pinball is the hero. Flip it into goblins, skeletons and ghosts, then pick your next room on the dungeon map: a fight, a tougher elite, treasure, a shop or a rest. Take relics that change how your ball plays, and beat Emberwing, the Gravelord and Abyssmaw to clear the dungeon. Gems you earn carry over between runs, so you can buy armory upgrades and unlock three more hero balls. After the last boss the laps keep going, so you can chase a high score.

**Do I need to download anything?** No. It's a free YouTube Playable, so it plays right in the YouTube app or your
browser, on phone or computer.
