Launch. Match five. Survive the surge.

Surge Blast is a quick-paced bubble matching game. Release bubbles onto the board and line up five of the same
color to pop them for points. Every eight bubbles a surge hits — a blast of mixed colors that scrambles your
plans and gets harder every time. Keep the board under control for as long as you can and beat your high score.

Everything in one place:
- Simple one-touch launching built for phones and tablets
- Match five bubbles of a color to pop them
- Surges every eight bubbles to keep the pressure climbing
- Chain pops for bigger scores
- Endless play with a saved high score
- A Game Center leaderboard to see where you rank
- Offline single player, no account needed
- Optional one-time Remove Ads purchase

Rebuilt and back on the App Store:
Surge Blast was rebuilt for current iPhones and iPads (iOS 15 or later) and given a black-and-neon look, with
the ads and privacy frameworks brought up to date. It is free with a small banner ad, and the NO ADS button
opens a one-time $2.99 Remove Ads purchase that turns off every ad for good. Restore Purchases is built in.
