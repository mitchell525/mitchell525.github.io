Aim, bank off the walls and launch glowing orbs into the pile. Match five of a color to pop them, and keep the pile from reaching the line, because every few shots a surge drops in a fresh wave. Power-ups unlock as you play, and there's a new daily run every day plus your records to beat.

**Do I need to download anything?** No. It's a free YouTube Playable, so it plays right in the YouTube app or your
browser, on phone or computer.

**On iPhone:** the App Store app is the earlier version of Surge Blast (bubble matching, black-and-neon look), free with
an optional one-time Remove Ads purchase.
