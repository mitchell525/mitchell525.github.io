Flip, fly, and survive the neon grid.

Pinball Overdrive blends classic arcade pinball with a retro neon look. Launch your ball into randomized levels full of hazards, power-ups, and score multipliers, and see how high you can climb.

### Everything in one place

 - Classic 2D pinball with realistic physics, built for touch
 - Randomized levels packed with hazards, bombs, and bonus pinballs
 - Power-ups and multipliers to boost your score
 - Survival Mode: one ball, one endless climb, with hazards that keep getting faster
 - 20 unlockable skins as you rack up points
 - Play fully offline—no Wi-Fi needed
 - Global leaderboards through Game Center

A new challenge: Survival Mode
Survival Mode strips it down to one ball and one goal: climb as high as you can before you lose it. Hazards come faster and more unpredictably the higher you get, and you'll unlock exclusive skins along the way just for climbing.

### Free to play, with an optional upgrade

Pinball Overdrive is free to download and play, supported by ads. A one-time Pro upgrade removes ads for good and unlocks Survival Mode—no subscription, ever.

**Is Pinball Overdrive free?** Yes. The core game is free to download and play, with ads to support development.

**What does the Pro upgrade include?** Pro is a one-time purchase that removes all ads and unlocks Survival Mode for good.

**Does it work offline?** Yes. Pinball Overdrive plays entirely offline, so it's great for flights and road trips.

**Can I compare scores with other players?** Yes. Sign in to Game Center to see your ranking on the global leaderboards.
