Beat the clock. Bolt on mods. See how far it goes.

Neon Mayhem is a roguelike arcade racer on an endless synthwave highway. Hold and drag to steer while your car flies
forward on its own. Weave through traffic, hit the ramps, smash the cones and reach the next checkpoint before time
runs out.

At every checkpoint you pick 1 of 3 mods, and each one bolts something you can see onto your car. Roof rockets that
blast the traffic ahead. Pogo springs that hop over cars. A spiked wrecking ball. Glider wings. An evil twin, a
hologram of your car that wrecks traffic on the other side of the road. Stack them into a build that's brilliant, or
gloriously broken.

Five zones, one endless road: start on Sunset Strip, then race through Midnight Grid, Toxic City, Vapor Coast and
Inferno Run, each with its own road, skyline and hazards.

- One thumb: hold and drag to steer
- 21 mods that change how your car looks and drives
- Near misses, big air, launched cars and combos for score
- Finish runs to unlock new mods and new rides, from the Party Van to the Big Rig
- A Game Center leaderboard for your best score
- Play in portrait or landscape
- Neon look and an original synthwave soundtrack
- Plays offline, no account needed

Fair ads, one optional purchase:
Ads only play when you choose them (to keep driving for 20 more seconds or reroll a mod offer, once a run each), plus
at most one short ad between runs, never during play and never in your first few minutes. The optional one-time
Supporter purchase removes the between-run ad, gives those rewards without ads, and adds the Sunset livery, a
pink-to-gold paint job for every car that you can switch off. It is a purchase, not a subscription, and Restore
Purchases is built in.
