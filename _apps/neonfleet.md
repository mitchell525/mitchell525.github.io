Start with one ship. End with an armada.

Neon Fleet is a roguelike space shooter where your fleet is your weapon. Drag to fly your flagship; every ship in
your fleet fires on its own. Tap anywhere to launch a missile salvo, grab salvage and rescue lost ships drifting in
the dark. Level up, pick 1 of 3 cards, and watch one ship become forty.

Every sector ends with a capital ship. The Warden, the Brood Carrier, the Twin Lancers, the Dreadnought and the Hive
Mother each fight their own way: find the glowing weak points and break them before the swarm breaks you.

Build a different fleet every run:
- Ships: interceptors, missile frigates, drone carriers, lancers and menders join the formation
- Systems, commands and officers: EMP Burst, the Nova Cannon, Overdrive and more
- Fusions and doctrines: commit to Swarm, Broadside or Siege for the big payoffs
- One thumb: drag to fly, tap to fire
- Five capital ships with weak points, then endless sectors
- Refit your flagship between runs and unlock new hulls
- A Game Center leaderboard for your best score
- Neon look and an original synthwave soundtrack
- Plays offline, no account needed

Fair ads, one optional purchase:
Ads only play when you choose them (to reroll a card offer, revive once a run, or double a run's cores), plus at most
one short ad between runs, never during play. The optional one-time Supporter purchase removes the between-run ad,
gives those rewards without ads, and paints your flagship in the Magenta Vanguard colours. It is a purchase, not a
subscription, and Restore Purchases is built in.
