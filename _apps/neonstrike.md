Neon Strike is an on-rails shooter. Fly a neon jet down the course, steer through the hazards and shoot down the enemy waves, picking an upgrade as you go. Every sector ends with its own giant boss, from the battleship Dreadnought VX-9 to the Magma Wyrm, the Sludge Stalker and the Star Reaper, and you beat each one by hitting its glowing weak points. Clear all seven sectors, then keep going for a higher score.

**Do I need to download anything?** No. It's a free YouTube Playable, so it plays right in the YouTube app or your
browser, on phone or computer.
