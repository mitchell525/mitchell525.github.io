One toy robot, six forms. Tap robot, car, jet, boat, drill or tank right before each obstacle and smash straight through it. Grab chips in the side lanes that stack up across a world, race five worlds of five races each, and beat the boss bot waiting at the end of every one. Finish by dropping onto the x10 pad. After the ending, flip to GOLD mode for twisted tracks, or stay on CLASSIC.

**Do I need to download anything?** No. It's a free YouTube Playable, so it plays right in the YouTube app or your
browser, on phone or computer.
