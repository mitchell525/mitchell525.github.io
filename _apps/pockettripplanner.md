Planning a trip should feel exciting, not overwhelming.

Pocket Trip Planner is a clean, ad-free trip planner that keeps your itineraries, packing lists, budgets, files, and countdowns in one simple, easy-to-use place. Whether you're planning a quick weekend getaway or a big family vacation, it keeps everything you need in one streamlined app so you can focus on enjoying your journey.

Everything in one place:
 - Create and manage trips with cover photos and countdowns
 - Build daily itineraries with events and activities
 - Plan transportation segments and travel days
 - Organize packing lists with reusable bag templates
 - Track spending with budget categories and transactions
 - Important Dates with reminders and notifications
 - Save confirmations, screenshots, and PDFs in Trip Files
 - Trip Summary, Notes, and polished PDF export
 - Home Screen countdown widget
 - Works on iPhone, iPad, and Apple Vision Pro
 - Ad-free, no account required

Smarter planning with Pro:
For travelers who want an extra hand, Pocket Trip Planner Pro adds optional on-device AI suggestions for daily plans and packing lists, AI-generated trip cover images via Image Playground, and automated event import from pasted itineraries, text files, PDFs, images, and photos. Pro can also save trip files from the Share Sheet and convert saved PDFs or images into editable events or transportation plans for review before adding them to your itinerary.

AI and import helpers are optional, live inside the Pro upgrade, and run privately on your device using Apple Intelligence and local text recognition — no account, no cloud.
