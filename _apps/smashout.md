You start inside a cardboard box. Walk into garden gnomes, flower pots, fences, BBQs and old cars to smash them for coins, then stand on the shop pads to get stronger, faster and longer-armed until you can punch your way out. From there it's floating islands: the Backyard, the Junkyard, Piñata Isle, Rooftop Town and Crystal Peak, plus hidden places tucked above and below them. Buy leaps, ground pounds and shockwaves, and when you're strong enough, smash the 40-metre statue of yourself to start again with a coin bonus, new outfits and, from the third time, the power to go giant.

**Do I need to download anything?** No. It's a free YouTube Playable, so it plays right in the YouTube app or your
browser, on phone or computer.
