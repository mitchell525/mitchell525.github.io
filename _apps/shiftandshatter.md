Shapes fall. Shift them into the matching color before they shatter — and it only gets faster.

Shift and Shatter is a quick-paced color matching game with six modes, each dressed in its own retro arcade
style. Drag every shape into the goal that matches its color, keep up as the pace climbs, and beat your own
best. Some modes add bombs, moving walls, corner goals or several shapes at once, so each one asks something
different of your reflexes.

Six modes:
- Standard — match colors as fast as you can
- Multi Match — several shapes drop at once
- Four Corners — a goal in every corner
- Avoid the Bombs — one wrong move ends the run
- Avoid the Walls — the walls are in the way
- All Together Now — everything at once

Everything in one place:
- Fast drag-and-drop color sorting built for touch
- Difficulty that ramps every round
- A Game Center leaderboard for each mode
- Offline single player with saved high scores
- Optional one-time Remove Ads purchase

Rebuilt and back on the App Store:
Shift and Shatter was rebuilt for current iPhones and iPads (iOS 15 or later), with the ads and privacy
frameworks brought up to date. It is free with a small banner ad, and the NO ADS button opens a one-time
$2.99 Remove Ads purchase that turns off every ad for good. Restore Purchases is built in.
