Each level is a little toy-town set piece: a farm, a gas station, a harbor, a fort. You get one tank of fuel, and every missile, meteor, wrecking ball swing and airstrike spends some of it. Find every target, hit the fuel tanks to start chain reactions, and see if you can flatten the whole place before the tank runs dry. Stars and upgrades come from what you knock down, and later worlds bring steel, flak and moving targets that make you pick the right power.

**Do I need to download anything?** No. It's a free YouTube Playable, so it plays right in the YouTube app or your
browser, on phone or computer.
