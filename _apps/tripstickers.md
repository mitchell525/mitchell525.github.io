Bring your adventures to life with Trip Stickers – the app that turns your travels into collectible stickers and journals. Inspired by the timeless style of vintage travel posters, Trip Stickers lets you capture memories in a creative way.

Create. Collect. Remember.
  - Design custom stickers in a bold, vintage travel style
  - Add a journal entry to record trip highlights and stories
  - Choose from curated icon packs inspired by camping, hiking, road trips, and more
  - Save your collection of travel memories all in one place

Go Premium for More
-  Export your stickers to use in scrapbooks, photo albums, or social media
- Unlock exclusive icon packs to personalize your travel collection even further

Whether you’re road tripping across the country, hiking a new trail, or exploring a hidden gem, Trip Stickers helps you remember every adventure with style.
