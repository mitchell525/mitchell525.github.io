Your magic fires on its own, so all you do is move. Walk out from camp into the Hexwood at night, where mushrooms, pumpkins, sprites and trolls come at you in crowds, and pick a new spell each time you level up. Mix two spells into a stronger one and fill your spellbook. Drop into crypts for relics, bring a pet along, and every trip home your camp grows a little. Four young witches and wizards to find, each with their own magic, three stages ending at the Hollow Oak, then the Endless Night for as long as you last.

**Do I need to download anything?** No. It's a free YouTube Playable, so it plays right in the YouTube app or your
browser, on phone or computer.
