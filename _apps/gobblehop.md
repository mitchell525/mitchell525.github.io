You are a little jelly slime on a board of glowing stone tiles. Hop onto a smaller slime to gobble it up and take its spot, and you grow. A bigger slime gobbles you instead, so every hop is a small puzzle. Adventure mode runs through new zones with upgrades and a slime boss at the end of each, and resets are always free. Endless mode is open from the start: no upgrades, every board solvable, just you and a high score.

**Do I need to download anything?** No. It's a free YouTube Playable, so it plays right in the YouTube app or your
browser, on phone or computer.
