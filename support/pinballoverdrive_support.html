Need help with Pinball Overdrive? This page covers the most common setup and purchase questions. Download the app on the App Store.

If you still need help, email mitchell525@gmail.com and include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the problem.

Requirements

  • Pinball Overdrive is built for iPhone and iPad
  • Free to download and play, supported by ads; an optional one-time Pro upgrade removes ads and unlocks Survival Mode (no subscription)
  • No account or sign-in is required to play
  • Signing in to Game Center is optional, and only needed to appear on the global leaderboards

Free and Pro

The free game includes the full classic pinball experience: randomized levels, hazards, power-ups, multipliers, offline play, and the classic-mode leaderboard—supported by ads.

Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It removes all ads for good and unlocks Survival Mode, along with its own set of exclusive skins and leaderboard.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I see ads?

Pinball Overdrive is free to download and play, and ads help support development. If you'd rather not see ads, the one-time Pro upgrade removes them for good.

I bought Pro but it's not unlocked

Open the Pro upgrade screen and use Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID used for the original purchase. If restore doesn't work, check your App Store purchase history for the Pro upgrade, then contact Apple Support for billing issues or email us with your device and iOS version.

How do I unlock Survival Mode?

Survival Mode is part of the Pro upgrade. Tap the locked Overdrive button on the home screen, or open the Pro upgrade screen, to purchase it.

My scores aren't showing up on the leaderboard

Make sure you're signed into Game Center and connected to the internet when you finish a run—scores are submitted to Game Center after each game. Classic mode and Survival Mode each have their own leaderboard.

Will I lose my skins or progress if I get a new phone?

High scores and unlocked skins are saved on your device. Your Pro purchase itself is tied to your Apple ID and can always be restored with Restore Purchase on a new device, but in-game progress does not currently transfer automatically between devices.

Purchases, Billing, and Refunds

Pinball Overdrive is free to download. The optional Pro upgrade is a one-time in-app purchase sold through the Apple App Store—not a subscription. Apple handles billing, purchase history, restore, and refunds. For charges, refund requests, or purchase problems, use Apple Support and your App Store purchase history. For restore or unlock issues after confirming your Apple ID purchase, email mitchell525@gmail.com.
