Need help with Pinball Defense Force? This page covers the most common questions. Download the app on the App Store.

If you still need help, email support@mitchsmith.app and include your device model, iOS version, and a short description of the problem.

Requirements

  • Pinball Defense Force is built for iPhone and iPad
  • Free to download and play, supported by ads—there are no in-app purchases or subscriptions
  • No account or sign-in is required to play
  • Signing in to Game Center is optional, and only needed to appear on the leaderboard

Ads and Continues

Pinball Defense Force is entirely free, with ads shown between games to support development. When you lose your last ball, you can watch a short ad to keep your run going instead of starting over.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I see ads?

Pinball Defense Force is free to download and play, and ads help support development. There's no purchase to remove them.

Are there any in-app purchases?

No. Pinball Defense Force doesn't include any purchases or subscriptions—the full game is free, supported by ads.

How does "continue" work?

When you lose your last ball, you'll be offered the option to watch a short ad and keep playing from where you left off, instead of starting a new game.

My score isn't showing up on the leaderboard

Make sure you're signed into Game Center and connected to the internet when your game ends—scores are submitted to the leaderboard automatically.

Will I lose my high score if I get a new phone?

Your best score is saved on your device, so it doesn't automatically transfer to a new device. If you're signed into Game Center, your leaderboard ranking stays tied to your Game Center account.
